# Filter Calibration

Iteration 2 rules that must be enforced:

1. Reject pure metaphor.
   `X is Zettelkasten` or `Y is a permanent note` is not enough.

2. Mechanism before similarity.
   The claim must explain a shared failure mode, lifecycle, architecture, or review mechanism.

3. New terms must expose an operation.
   A good term leads to a checklist, schema, workflow, experiment, or implementation decision.

4. Same-domain cross-project patterns are valid.
   Do not force artificial cross-domain jumps when the stronger insight is cross-project mechanism reuse.

5. Positive exemplars:
   - `Semantic Drift`
   - `Silent Consumer Drift`
   - `Oracle Scarcity Architecture`
   - `Raw -> Candidate -> Canonical -> Adopted`
   - `Downstream-Invariant Evaluation`

Anti-filter:

- reject `both`, `similar`, `related` without mechanism
- reject claims writable from a single source
- reject polished summaries that do not create a new operational surface
